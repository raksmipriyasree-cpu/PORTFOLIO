IBCP CORE / COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

Twenty-one days of teaching embroidery.

A community experience built around sharing a practical skill, learning through interaction and understanding that meaningful engagement is a two-way process.

THE EXPERIENCE

Teaching became more than teaching.

I spent 21 days teaching embroidery as a community-engagement experience. The programme was designed around practical embroidery skills, peer support, reflection and creative exploration, with an AI-integrated design component.

The programme itself moves from basic setup and stitching techniques into sampler work, AI-assisted design, pattern selection and a final creative project. The supporting programme document is ready to sit beside the story.

THE 21-DAY ARC

From first stitches to creative work.

The uploaded programme gives the experience a clear progression from foundations to independent creative application.

01–06

Foundations & practice

Introduction to embroidery, running stitch, back stitch, chain stitch, lazy daisy and French knot, followed by a sampler project and peer support.

07–09

AI meets craft

Exploring AI-assisted design, generating and analysing embroidery ideas, selecting designs and transferring them to fabric.

10+

Build, document & reflect
